Seth Timmons

Classification: Surprisingly Competent · Sector: Sol III · Region: Idaho (Mountain Time)

A bipedal, carbon-based life form stationed in the digital infrastructure of DualEnroll, an Earth-based educational technology concern, where he holds the designation Workflow & Integrations Engineer. His primary function is persuading computer systems that were never designed to communicate to exchange data accurately, repeatedly, and without complaint.

Prior to this posting he spent thirteen years at Idaho State University, rising from a one-person web operation to the senior analyst responsible for the institution's enterprise integrations, application architecture, and the general wellbeing of most things with a blinking light.

Observed off-duty behaviours include tabletop worldbuilding under version control, modifying video games that did not ask to be modified, and building small Android applications with the aid of a machine intelligence.
