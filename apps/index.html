CYPHER

A freestyle-rap trainer. Random words drop in on a timer with a sweep bar counting down the bar, over a beat you choose.

  • Tap the word when you've used it; tap the right edge to skip. Tracks said / shown.
  • Words from an open random-word API, with a built-in list when you're offline. Filter by length.
  • Beats: a boom-bap loop synthesized live with the Web Audio API (adjustable BPM), your own audio file, or a YouTube link.

Ricochet Song

A bounce puzzle you solve by ear. Turn the paddles so the ball rings each coloured pad on its beat, in the tune's order, then reaches the flag.

  • 20 tunes in five sets, each adding one idea: crossing routes, bolted paddles, a switch that flips paddles, turns mid-roll, duets.
  • Ear mode hides every colour and letter, so you match the tune by listening.
  • A level studio: build your own boards, group them into courses, share them as a code.
