The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy — Earth Edition — Entry #7,742,112
Seth Timmons
Classification: Surprisingly Competent · Sector: Sol III (Earth) · Region: Classified
Fig. 1: Artist's rendering of his last appearance.

Seth Timmons is a bipedal carbon-based life form primarily stationed in the digital infrastructure of a small Earth-based educational technology concern called DualEnroll, where he holds the title of Workflow and Integrations Engineer—a designation the Guide's editorial department required three attempts and a stiff Pan Galactic Gargle Blaster to properly file.

His primary function involves convincing disparate computer systems to communicate with one another, a task roughly as straightforward as mediating a territorial dispute between a Vogon constructor fleet and a bypass-averse planet. He achieves this through an arsenal of automation pipelines, integration frameworks, and what colleagues describe as "an alarming tolerance for YAML."

Editor's Note: Despite initial survey classifications, the Guide's research department has revised the 'Mostly Harmless' designation to 'Surprisingly Competent,' citing over thirteen Earth-years of continuous infrastructure management and an improbable ability to debug production issues at 3 AM while maintaining conversational pleasantness.

Prior to his current posting, Timmons spent thirteen years at a sprawling Earth institution known as Idaho State University, where he rose through the ranks from a one-man development operation to a senior analyst responsible for enterprise integrations, application architecture, and the general well-being of approximately everything with a blinking light.

He is known to enjoy tabletop role-playing games (a curious Earth ritual involving dice and collective storytelling), Raspberry Pi projects (small computing devices named after neither raspberries nor pies), and building tools that help people practice freestyle rapping (an Earth art form the Guide's cultural department is still attempting to classify).
